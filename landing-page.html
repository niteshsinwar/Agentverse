Pricing FAQs

What's the licensing model?

AgentVerse is a licensed SaaS platform. All plans grant you usage rights via subscription. You don't purchase ownership or reselling rights. Contact sales for partnership inquiries.

Do I need my own LLM API keys?

Yes. AgentVerse connects to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google on your behalf. You pay LLM costs directly to them - we only charge for the platform.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts, no penalties. Cancel with one click. Your data stays accessible for 30 days after cancellation.

What counts as an "AI call"?

Each message sent to an agent or tool execution counts as 1 call. The Free plan has 500/day (generous for testing). Pro is unlimited.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes! Pay yearly and get 2 months free (17% off). Pro becomes $490/year instead of $588. Contact us for Enterprise pricing.

Can I deploy on my own infrastructure?

Enterprise licenses include self-hosted deployment options for full data control. You still subscribe to AgentVerse - ownership remains with AgentVerse.
